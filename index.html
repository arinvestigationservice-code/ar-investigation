FAQ

Common questions.

What information do you need to start a case?

The applicant's name, address or business details, contact number, the type of verification required and any supporting documents or instructions. More detail helps us deliver faster and more accurately.

How long does a report take?

Turnaround depends on the type of case, location and scope. We confirm the expected timeline when we accept the case.

What are your charges?

Charges depend on the service, location and effort involved. Contact us with your requirement and we will share a quote before starting.

Is my case kept confidential?

Yes. Case information and findings are shared only with the client who assigned the case.

Which areas do you cover?

Contact us with the location of your case and we will confirm availability.
