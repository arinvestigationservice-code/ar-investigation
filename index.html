ABOUT A R INVESTIGATION

Investigation with a method.

A R Investigation is focused on professional field-level investigation and verification support for businesses and organizations.

Our approach combines field visits, information gathering, observation and structured reporting to help clients make informed decisions.

“Every case deserves facts that can be clearly understood and acted upon.”

Lawful methods only

All work is carried out within the law. We do not obtain call records, bank details or other protected data by illegal means.

Confidential handling

Case details are shared only with the client. See our Privacy Policy.

Structured reports

Findings are consolidated into a clear report, so decisions can be made without guesswork.

FAQ

Common questions.

What information do you need to start a case?

The subject's name, address or business details, the type of verification required and any supporting documents or instructions. More detail helps us deliver faster and more accurately.

How long does a report take?

Turnaround depends on the type of case, location and scope. We confirm the expected timeline when we accept the case.

What are your charges?

Charges depend on the service, location and effort involved. Contact us with your requirement and we will share a quote before starting.

Is my case kept confidential?

Yes. Case information and findings are shared only with the client who assigned the case.

Which areas do you cover?

Contact us with the location of your case and we will confirm availability.

Is there anything you will not do?

We work strictly within the law. We do not carry out illegal surveillance or obtain call records, bank statements or other protected data by unlawful means. See our Disclaimer.
