From case to report

A clear process, so you know what happens after you send us a case.

  1. 1

    Case received

    You send the case details and what needs to be checked. We review the requirements and the information available, and confirm scope and charges with you before any visit.

  2. 2

    Field visit

    Our team goes to the location and verifies on the ground according to the assigned scope: observation, local enquiry and the checks you asked for.

  3. 3

    Findings

    Relevant observations and information are consolidated and cross-checked against the details you gave us.

  4. 4

    Final report

    You receive clear findings in a structured report, shared only with you.

Common questions

What information do you need to start a case?

The applicant's name, address or business details, contact number, the type of verification required and any supporting documents or instructions. More detail helps us deliver faster and more accurately.

How long does a report take?

Turnaround depends on the type of case, location and scope. We confirm the expected timeline when we accept the case.

What are your charges?

Charges depend on the service, location and effort involved. Contact us with your requirement and we will share a quote before starting.

Is my case kept confidential?

Yes. Case information and findings are shared only with the client who assigned the case.

Which areas do you cover?

We are based in Agra, Uttar Pradesh. Contact us with the location of your case and we will confirm availability.

Have a case that needs checking on the ground?

Tell us what needs to be verified and where. We reply with scope and a quote before any work starts.
