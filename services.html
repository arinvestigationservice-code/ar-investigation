Residence verification

Confirms that a person lives at the address they gave, and records what is visible on the ground.

What we look at

  • Whether the person is present at, or known to live at, the given address
  • Type of house and signs of occupancy, as observed
  • Locality, nearby landmarks and how easy the address is to find
  • Feedback from neighbours or locals, where available
Ask about residence verification

Office verification

Checks that an office exists at the stated address and is actually in use.

What we look at

  • Office at the given address, with signage or name boards where present
  • Staff presence and day-to-day activity during the visit
  • Setup of the premises: seating, equipment, records
  • Whether the person we are asked about is known at that office
Ask about office verification

Business verification

Assesses whether a business exists, operates and looks the way it has been described.

What we look at

  • Existence of the business at the stated premises
  • Nature of activity and how the premises are used
  • Stock, equipment and visible scale of operations
  • Local enquiry about how long and how actively it operates
Ask about business verification

Employment verification

Field checks on whether a person works where they say they do.

What we look at

  • Employer's presence at the given address
  • Confirmation of employment through the contacts specified in your instructions
  • Designation and joining details, as told by the employer
  • A structured record of who confirmed what
Ask about employment verification

Document verification

Compares the details in a person's documents with what we find on the ground.

What we look at

  • Names, addresses and other details in the documents against field findings
  • Mismatches and points that need a closer look
  • A clear list of what matched and what did not
Ask about document verification

Custom field checks

Other on-ground checks, set up around your instructions.

What we look at

  • Scope agreed with you before the visit
  • Reporting in the format you need
  • Quote shared before we start
