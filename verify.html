Verify an A R Investigation ID

Check that a field executive or coordinator really works with us. Scan the QR code on their ID card, or enter the ID number printed on it.

What the result means

We show the person's name, role, location, joining date, photo and current status. We never show contact numbers or addresses.

Active
Currently works with A R Investigation and is authorised to act for us.
Left
No longer works with us. Not authorised.
Terminated or blocked
Not authorised to act for us in any way.
No record
We have no staff member with that ID. Do not treat the person as our representative.

If the result is anything other than Active, or you are unsure, email ar.investigation.service@gmail.com.
