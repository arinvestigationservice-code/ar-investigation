About A R Investigation

A field verification team based in Agra, Uttar Pradesh, working for businesses and organisations that need facts from the ground.

What we do

A R Investigation is focused on professional field-level verification support for businesses and organisations.

Our approach combines field visits, information gathering, observation and structured reporting, so that clients receive accurate and timely verification reports.

We work from Agra, Uttar Pradesh. For a case in another location, send us the details and we will confirm whether we can take it.

How we work

We report what we observe

Reports separate what was seen or confirmed from what was only stated. We do not fill gaps with guesses.

Your case stays yours

Case details and findings are shared only with the client who assigned the case.

Scope before work

We agree what will be checked, and the charges, before a field visit begins.

Staff you can check

Every field executive and coordinator carries an ID card that anyone can check on our verification page.

Want to know if we can take your case?

Send the type of verification and the location. We will confirm and share a quote.
